Hallucinations and Evasions: Red Teaming with AI-Generated Malware
Speaker: Alejandro Torres
This session reverse-engineers the prompt engineering behind modern adversary simulation. We demonstrate how carefully crafted instructions bypass safety filters to generate functional tradecraft—from infostealers to modular C2 frameworks with persistence and process injection.
We analyze which AI architectures resist malicious generation versus which collapse under iterative prompting, testing capabilities across commercial and open-source models. Live demonstrations include evasion of microsoft Defender solutions and achieving zero detections on popular scanning platforms.
All demonstrated techniques are confined to authorized red team operations
